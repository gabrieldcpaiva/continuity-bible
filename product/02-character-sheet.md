> **Tip — What makes a good variant line**  
> Three to five structural differences someone could see from the other side of the room: what is open, what is pushed up, what is carried, what colour block has appeared. Fine detail drifts; silhouette holds.

## Status markers used on this sheet

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| F | Fixed — Locked for the whole sequence. Copy it into every shot word for word. |
| C | Changed intentionally — This shot deliberately differs from the one before it. The change has a code. |
| U | Unresolved — You have not decided yet. It is allowed to stay open, but not silently. |
| N/A | Not applicable — The field does not apply to this shot. Say why in three words. |

## Before you move on

- Could a stranger with only this page tell whether the last frame was the same person? If not, written the anchors are not structural enough.
- Does every anchor survive a wide shot? Anything only visible in a close-up belongs in Watch-outs on the shot table.
- Is there exactly one V1 row, and does every later row have a cause you could actually shoot or skip?
- Does the anchor line name a frame side or a hand for anything that could flip? Left, right, east, west — pick one and stay with it.
