## 2. The checklist post (no link required)

> **Title:** The 15 checks I run before I press generate (steal this)
>
> The worked example in the free sheets is an invented scene. Six shots, one room. Between shot 4 and shot 5 the mug tips, and the sheet has to say so before the generation, or the next shot puts it back upright and full. That mug is the example. It is not a story from my own week.
>
> The fix is a list you can read in sixty seconds, in the same order, every time:
>
> 1. **Row check** — the shot number and slug match the row I'm working from.
> 2. **Identity** — the five face anchors are pasted word for word, same order.
> 3. **Never-change list** — three character items and three location items are in the prompt.
> 4. **Variant code** — the code matches the previous shot, or the change has a code and a cause.
> 5. **Wardrobe** — same garment set, same fastening state, same sleeve length.
> 6. **Hair** — same part, same tuck, same length.
> 7. **Hands and props** — correct hand, correct prop state, the other hand accounted for.
> 8. **Prop state** — mug, paper, tools and carried items match the row.
> 9. **Geography** — two landmarks named with their frame side.
> 10. **The line** — the camera stays on the same side of the line, or the cross has a written reason.
> 11. **Light state** — the light code matches, including what is off.
> 12. **Time band** — nothing in frame records an impossible time.
> 13. **Framing** — size, height, angle and movement match the row.
> 14. **Handoff** — say the handoff line out loud.
> 15. **Open items** — any unresolved mark is decided, or its accepted risk is written down.
>
> Steal the list. The full sheets, the PDF and the worked example are free: see the other post for the link.

## 4. Usability DMs

> I built a free continuity worksheet for six-shot scenes and I need two people to use the blank sheets cold. 40 minutes, on a call. I give you a one-line scene, you plan six shots, I stay quiet unless you're stuck. Want one of the slots?

Follow up once, three days later, and then stop.

Record, the same day: who, whether they named a real sequence, and the first field they skipped. A compliment is not a result.
