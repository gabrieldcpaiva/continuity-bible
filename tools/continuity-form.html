The Continuity Bible · v1.0

Offline continuity form

The same fields as the printed sheets, in one page that works with no connection, no account and no install. Fill it in, then use Build the handoff to turn it into plain text you can keep, print or paste into your notes.

Unsaved text is lost on reload. A saved draft stays only in this browser, and it is never sent anywhere.

Status markers. Every field you may need to mark takes one of four letters: F fixed · C changed intentionally (name the variant code) · U unresolved (say what you will accept) · N/A not applicable (say why). The letter is the marker; colour is never the only signal.
